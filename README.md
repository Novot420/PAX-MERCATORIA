PAX MERCATORIA

Freedom through voluntary exchange.

PAX MERCATORIA is an independent project focused on building an open, privacy-conscious marketplace infrastructure based on individual liberty, private property and voluntary exchange.

Our Mission

PAX MERCATORIA exists to enable voluntary exchange between free individuals.

Founding Principles

Individual Liberty — Respect for individual sovereignty and freedom of choice.
Voluntary Exchange — Every transaction must be based on mutual consent.
Private Property — Respect for legitimate ownership.
Privacy — Protection of individual privacy and autonomy.
Trust and Accountability — Building trust through transparent actions and responsible conduct.
Independence — Striving for open-source development and technological independence.
Project Vision

The long-term vision is to create an international peer-to-peer marketplace ecosystem supporting the exchange of goods and services through independent, privacy-conscious and cryptocurrency-native infrastructure.

Development Status

Early Development — Foundation Stage

The project is currently in its initial development phase. Its foundational structure and technical environment are being established.

No production marketplace or payment infrastructure is currently available.

Philosophy

PAX MERCATORIA is built around one fundamental principle:

Freedom through voluntary exchange.
